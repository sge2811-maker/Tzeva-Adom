Environment & lighting

Building

Skyline, atmosphere, fog, tone mapping, bloom, SSAO, colour grade

Critic R3: 5/10 (2 rounds without improvement; round 4 is the last before flagging). S4/S5 near 6–7; wide frames (S1, S2, S3, S6, S7) still read as generated: octagon core and racetrack villa loop, empty beige foreground, cable-like far roads, straight plateau rim.

Open issues 5

  • blockerAt the default tactical camera, Arad is still an octagon of equal square blocks with glowing tan outlines on a blank plain. The new 'villa quarter' to the east is a perfect ellipse of red-roofed houses around a loop lane, with a straight spur in. From above it reads as a racetrack or a stadium, which is the most procedural-looking object in the frame. The west lobe is a thin Y of sheds, and the walled compounds are lone square tiles. In S6 the town is still a band of silver-white boxes, not warm windows on dark massing. Next to Age of Empires IV and CS2 overviews, this is a first-glance prototype tell.
  • majorThree of the seven canonical frames are about 60% empty flat beige plane. S1's foreground is a uniform featureless surface, with nothing between the camera and the town. In S3 the plateau to the horizon is a single flat tone with no rocks, wadis, scrub or relief. In S7 the bottom two-thirds is an even grey-brown sheet. The claimed shrubs and desert-pavement mottling are invisible at these distances. Iron Harvest and CoH3 never leave half the screen as a flat albedo, and TW:WH3 foregrounds always carry rock, relief or vegetation.
  • majorThe regional roads render as blue-black tubes that look raised above the ground. In S7 the Route 31 line and the long diagonal look like cables lying on the desert. In S1 a dark navy rope curves in from the left. The town.js comment says far asphalt should read as a pale grey line, but in the shots it is the darkest value in the scene, and it is cold blue against the warm ground.
  • majorThe plateau still ends in a ruler-straight horizontal line against the far ridge in S3 and S6. There is no visible drop, rift haze or cliff band, so the mid-ground layer (escarpment and Dead Sea) is missing from both frames, and the composition goes straight from flat ground to the ridge. In S6 the far ridge is a flat uniform band, and above it is a huge empty black sky.
  • minorRoofs are still flat grey slabs with a single box and a red dot, repeated across buildings. The rooftop clutter claimed to be bigger is not readable at S4 zoom. The block interiors are flat brown, and the scrub decals are square yellow-green tiles with hard edges. The battery vehicles in S3 are simple box clusters.

VFX

Building

Launches, interceptor trails, intercepts, debris, smoke, screen shake

Critic R3: 7/10 (better in absolute terms; 1 round without a higher score). Critic: fixing the dark 'egg' columns, near-camera re-entry pillars and the between-kills S6 frame makes 8 plausible. Round 4 building.

Open issues 7

  • majorIn C5 (every frame, x~488 y~250 at 960) and S9 (x~815 y~435), an earlier impact column renders as a solid, hard-edged, dark rounded egg about 30x40 px with no wisps, no shear and no fire light. It is the most obvious 'sprite-looking puff / hard-edged circle' failure sign in the slice, and it sits next to the hero impact in the canonical Nevatim shot. CoH3 and WARNO distant columns are tall, tapered and translucent, sheared by wind.
  • majorC4 f00097: a nearby ballistic hot trail curls into a bright orange J-hook at the left edge. C4 f00108: the same object becomes a full-height thin vertical orange pillar with a pale column beside it and a broad warm wash over the left third of the frame. Width capping helped, but the pillar and hook remain. They read as a rendering glitch, not a meteor.
  • majorAfter each endo kill the lingering smoke is still an isolated, round, soft grey puff about 30-45 px across at 1600 (S5 x~1145 y~370, C4 x~683 y~225 in f00097 and f00108). They float alone, well away from the explosion, with a near-circular outline. CoH3 and WARNO post-kill smoke is torn, directional and connected to the burst.
  • majorS6 (t=222.7) still lands between kills. The store-page frame for 'Arrow 3 over the Dead Sea' shows about ten short orange streaks falling in parallel, like spent fireworks, with no star and no visible remnant smear. In C1 the same drizzle surrounds every star (f00030, f00084). Broken Arrow's exo kills leave a bright point plus a faint expanding smudge, not a shower of orange rockets.
  • minorS7 from the high tactical camera: Nevatim has one dark column about 25-30 px wide (x~990 y~240 at 1600) with a small fire base. Along the escarpment there is a row of 8-10 near-identical pale puffs, each with a bright point at its base, evenly spaced. The row reads as procedural repetition. CoH3 aftermath frames are dominated by a few big, wind-sheared columns of varied size.
  • minorIn C1 and S6, the Arrow boost-phase trails still appear only as wide, very faint blue vertical bands on the right side of the sky (C1 f00030, x 650-850). They read as aurora or searchlights, not as lit missile smoke.

HUD & UX

Building

Alert countdown, objectives, shelter routing — cinematic and minimal

Critic R2: 7/10 (fixes verified; 1 round without a higher score). Edge pointers float mid-screen, chip pile on group select, open shelters lose capacity at default zoom, radar town plot unreadable, HUD 2.5 ms/frame. Round 3 building.

Open issues 6

  • majorAt the siren (C2 f00040–f00056), the 'WEST LOCKED' and '10 LOCKED OUT' pointers sit in open world space at x≈190–370, not on a screen edge. They are clamped to an inner left bound of 290*mkScale past the roster, or to the projected foot of an 'under' marker. Each pill is the largest element on screen after the alert box, about twice the height of a world chip, and its direction arrow is a 5 px triangle. A player reads them as world labels sitting on random buildings, not as 'over there'. The real Lookout lock and group are visible top-left as a dot plus a '16' chip, so the same trouble shows twice. AoE IV and CoH3 put off-screen alerts flush against the viewport border as small icon tabs, and the minimap carries the ping.
  • majorSelecting a group (ux-1, the core routing action) forceFulls every shelter (m.forceFull = !!sel). The town centre becomes a stack of 5–6 long text chips ('LOCKED 260s CLICK: OPEN + SEND', '0/40 248s 32 FREE', '0/28 383s 16 FREE') lifted 24 px above feet that sit close together. Which stem and foot belong to which chip is ambiguous: the 0/40 chip and the 0/28 chip both hover over the same cluster of red diamonds. Unselected groups drop to icon-only dots with no count. CoH3 and AoE IV show a compact cost or ETA tag pinned exactly on the target and keep the long help text in the tooltip or coach line.
  • minorIn S4b-alert-wide and S2, open shelters are bare icons with no capacity. The brief asks for shelter markers that show capacity, and at the alert a player choosing where to reroute cannot see '0/40' vs '0/12' without zooming or selecting a group. The open shelter icon often sits under its group's chip (S4b: '10 21s' chip over its shelter at 632,378), so the pair reads as one blob. The builder's own gaps (open shelters collapsing to dots mid-zoom, locked S1/S5 as dots at the siren) are confirmed: ux-test reports 'marker dot' for both S1 and S5 when clicked.
  • minorThe radar now plots shelters and groups, but at 2 px shelter squares and 1.5 px group dots on a 226×118 map where Arad spans a few pixels, the town is an unreadable speck (S2, S4). The locked-shelter ping is the only usable signal. WARNO and Broken Arrow minimaps use clearly sized, shape-coded contacts.
  • minorFeed timestamps (10 px, --ink-faint #627580) are still barely legible over bright terrain (S2 bottom right, '23:46'). The feed stacks to the screen edge with no bottom margin matching the command bar. A marker chip pokes out at the left screen edge behind the roster (blue square at x=0 in C2 f00056). Between the siren and the auto-unlock, the Lookout locked shelter in C2 f00040 is a red lock dot jammed between the clock and roster panels.
  • minorIn S7 (all clear, no town hits) 'No impacts in Arad' still shows '0' with no check while the other three are ticked. The debrief then shows it as done, which is inconsistent. capture.json reports hud update 2.5 ms on the sampled frame against a 4 ms whole-frame JS budget, which is too much for a DOM overlay.
