Environment & lighting

Flagged

Skyline, atmosphere, fog, tone mapping, bloom, SSAO, colour grade

FLAGGED after 4 critic rounds at 5/10 (3 rounds without improvement). Street-level frames (S4/S5) reach ~6–7 and the S7 Moab vista is strong; wide RTS frames still read procedural: octagon town core, trident lanes, invisible rock/acacia scatter (bug: all instances land in one strip), silver far town, navy regional roads. Open issues listed for a follow-up pass.

Open issues 5

  • blockerAt the default tactical camera, Arad is still a near-perfect octagon of equal square blocks, each outlined by a bright tan walk strip. From S7 it reads as a glowing coin, and from S2 as a game board. The racetrack loop is gone, but the replacement villa quarter and the west industrial area both read from above as symmetric forked 'tridents' or feather shapes (a spine that splits into 2-3 short prongs with a single row of houses on each). That is just as procedural a tell. The NE slab quarter and west sheds are thin strings, not neighbourhoods. Age of Empires IV and CS2 overviews never show a town edge this geometric.
  • majorThe ~700 outcrops and ~900 acacias are invisible in every canonical frame (two lone 'lollipop' trees in S1). S3 is still a uniform beige plane from the camera to a ruler-straight horizon at the plateau rim, with the battery pad as a raised hard-edged beige slab. S6 shows the same straight rim, with the town sitting on a featureless sheet. TW:WH3 and Iron Harvest foregrounds always carry rock, relief or vegetation. Here half the screen is still empty.
  • majorDarkening the far facades to 0.25 did not fix S6: the town is still a band of silver-white boxes. The culprit is the window term, not the albedo. Far windows get 2.2x gain (town.js line ~452), and their mix of cool and white temperatures averages, at sub-pixel size, to a cold silver sheen. CS2 skylines at distance read as warm amber specks on dark massing.
  • majorRoute 31 in S7 and the approach road in S1 are still the darkest, coldest value in the frame: a navy rope that looks raised above the desert. The new 'blend toward desert at distance' is in the town street shader (town.js line 166) and does not seem to reach the regional road ribbons, or it is overridden by fog and specular.
  • minorS6's upper two-thirds is near-black with faint vertical blue streaks on the right, which read as banding or ray artefacts rather than sky. In S4, roofs are still flat grey slabs with one box and a red dot, repeated; lots are flat brown; scrub decals are square camo-like tiles with hard edges; and the S3 battery vehicles are box clusters.

VFX

Flagged

Launches, interceptor trails, intercepts, debris, smoke, screen shake

Flagged at 7 after final round 5 (r4 7, r5 7). Sky strobe on overhead kills and the near-camera trail wedge go into the integration pass.

Open issues 6

  • majorEach overhead Sling or ballistic kill flips the entire sky from near-black to a flat, uniform grey-lavender. The stars vanish and every building facade lights a flat cream, with no direction toward the burst. The burst itself is a small orange fireball at the top right. Sky mean luminance jumps from 1.5 to 51-54 and decays over about 0.3 s, and it fires about 7 times between t=252.2 and 254.7. It reads as exposure strobing or lightning, not as a flare-bright intercept lighting the smoke. Iron Harvest and CoH3 flashes light locally and directionally, with falloff.
  • majorA pale, translucent, triangular band with a ruler-straight right edge runs from the top-centre sky down through the Nevatim impact and into the foreground ground (S9 x~800-1300, y 0-600 at 1600). It is in every C5 frame from about t=256. It is a smoke ribbon (most likely the David's Sling Nev. West launch trail beside the 11/12 marker) seen nearly end-on near the camera, so the billboarded strip becomes a huge flat polygon. It reads as a searchlight beam or a rendering glitch beside the hero impact. This is the 'hard-edged' and 'flat sprite' failure sign in TARGET §2.
  • majorIn C1 the Arrow 3 kills land at the top-centre of the frame (about y 30-110 at 960), mostly behind the red alert panel. They are about 10-15 px soft stars at roughly the same brightness as the panel glow (f00049-f00090 zoom). The 16-sprite remnant haze added this round doesn't show at all. The canonical S6-salvo frame (t=222.7) is now essentially empty night sky: one small star under the alert panel (x~890 y~195) and faint aurora bands. The signature 'bright points high over the Dead Sea' moment from Broken Arrow's high-altitude SAM kills does not reach the player.
  • minorLong, perfectly straight, hairline trails cross the sky diagonally in S5 (x 850-1310, y 330-650, blue-grey fading to orange) and in C4 f00065/f00092 (several thin lines spanning a third of the frame). At street level they read as lines drawn on the sky, not as plasma or smoke. This is the 'trails as thin lines' failure sign. WARNO and Broken Arrow trails have width, taper and wind wobble.
  • minorS7 from the high camera is mostly empty desert. Two matched pairs of identical bright flares with vertical spikes sit on the escarpment (x 840-860 and 945-965, y ~195-205) and still read as procedural copies. A field of thin vertical light streaks runs along the far shore. One modest column on the right (about 25 px wide) is the only real smoke. The CoH3 aftermath bar is several lingering, drifting palls of varied size over the battlefield.
  • minorThis is unchanged. In S6 and C1 the Arrow boost trails are still very wide, very faint blue vertical bands on the right side of the sky (S6 x 1150-1500), reading as aurora rather than missile smoke.

Audio

Building

Siren timing, spatial audio, impacts, ambience (original/synthesized only)

Critic R1: 6/10 (judged from spectrograms, measurements and code; nobody has listened). Siren contour, speed-of-sound timing and mix hygiene strong; barrage has no weight, siren level fixed at alert start, 808-style sine bodies, silent re-entries. Round 2 building.

Open issues 10

  • blockerThe barrage has no weight. With the trailer camera across the salvo (200-240 s), 17 intercepts and impacts leave short-term loudness flat at about -28 LUFS (LRA 3.6 LU). The biggest event, an impact at 1.8 km, adds only +3.7 dB broadband. Intercepts at 5-8 km add 0 to +2 dB. In the street render (205-232 s), no boom raises the broadband level by more than 0.9 dB under the siren. The loudest thing in the mission is the siren, at -16 LUFS short-term. In CoH3 and WARNO, explosions punch 10-20 LU above the bed. Here the climax plays as a steady siren drone.
  • majorSiren level and air filtering are frozen at the listener distance at alert start. E.siren() sets g.gain = distGain(d) and air = airCut(d) once, and the panner has rolloffFactor 0. Panning a 75 s siren from Arad out to Nevatim (7 km), or flying the trailer camera, never changes its loudness or brightness. In the trailer render, the Arad poles stay at a constant -23.5 dB with the d=2.4 km start distance. This fails TARGET §3: 'sources pan and attenuate correctly as the camera moves.' Long launch voices (Arrow 8.5 s) are frozen at the pad position in the same way.
  • majorBlast and launch bodies are sine sweeps: crack() adds a sine at 150→42 Hz, and blast() adds a sine at 58→22 Hz. This is the classic 808-kick tell, and every launch and every boom carries the same tonal 'doom' shape. The code comment promises an N-wave, but crack() is a high-passed white-noise spit, not an N-wave.
  • majorThe tension bed is pure sines: 55, 55.35 and 82.41 Hz, plus static triangle chords at the all-clear. In the post-siren lull (238-246 s), the 55 Hz line alone is -37 dB against a total of -32.6 dB, so a single pure sine carries about 36% of the energy. The 82.8 Hz line adds another -40 dB. It is visible as a constant bright band across every spectrogram. On headphones or a sub, this reads as an electrical hum, not a score.
  • majorHigh-frequency loss with distance is too gentle. airCut() feeds a single 12 dB/oct biquad, and the crack's 'near' term reaches out to 4.4 km. An impact at 2.7 km therefore still prints a crack up to 16 kHz (white line at 209.8 s in the salvo spectrogram), and Sling launches at 1 km show crackle up to 16 kHz. Real air absorption removes nearly everything above about 4 kHz by 2-3 km.
  • majorIncoming ballistic missiles make no sound until impact. The recorder skips th.type === 'ballistic' (index.js:145), and nothing synthesizes the re-entry. The tearing roar and sonic-boom double crack of descending re-entry vehicles is the defining sound of the Nevatim strikes. Jets are generic filtered noise, without the ground-reflection comb 'flange' that makes a flyby read as a jet.

HUD & UX

Critic reviewing

Alert countdown, objectives, shelter routing — cinematic and minimal

r3 builder done; critic running

Open issues 6

  • majorAt the siren (C2 f00040–f00056), the 'WEST LOCKED' and '10 LOCKED OUT' pointers sit in open world space at x≈190–370, not on a screen edge. They are clamped to an inner left bound of 290*mkScale past the roster, or to the projected foot of an 'under' marker. Each pill is the largest element on screen after the alert box, about twice the height of a world chip, and its direction arrow is a 5 px triangle. A player reads them as world labels sitting on random buildings, not as 'over there'. The real Lookout lock and group are visible top-left as a dot plus a '16' chip, so the same trouble shows twice. AoE IV and CoH3 put off-screen alerts flush against the viewport border as small icon tabs, and the minimap carries the ping.
  • majorSelecting a group (ux-1, the core routing action) forceFulls every shelter (m.forceFull = !!sel). The town centre becomes a stack of 5–6 long text chips ('LOCKED 260s CLICK: OPEN + SEND', '0/40 248s 32 FREE', '0/28 383s 16 FREE') lifted 24 px above feet that sit close together. Which stem and foot belong to which chip is ambiguous: the 0/40 chip and the 0/28 chip both hover over the same cluster of red diamonds. Unselected groups drop to icon-only dots with no count. CoH3 and AoE IV show a compact cost or ETA tag pinned exactly on the target and keep the long help text in the tooltip or coach line.
  • minorIn S4b-alert-wide and S2, open shelters are bare icons with no capacity. The brief asks for shelter markers that show capacity, and at the alert a player choosing where to reroute cannot see '0/40' vs '0/12' without zooming or selecting a group. The open shelter icon often sits under its group's chip (S4b: '10 21s' chip over its shelter at 632,378), so the pair reads as one blob. The builder's own gaps (open shelters collapsing to dots mid-zoom, locked S1/S5 as dots at the siren) are confirmed: ux-test reports 'marker dot' for both S1 and S5 when clicked.
  • minorThe radar now plots shelters and groups, but at 2 px shelter squares and 1.5 px group dots on a 226×118 map where Arad spans a few pixels, the town is an unreadable speck (S2, S4). The locked-shelter ping is the only usable signal. WARNO and Broken Arrow minimaps use clearly sized, shape-coded contacts.
  • minorFeed timestamps (10 px, --ink-faint #627580) are still barely legible over bright terrain (S2 bottom right, '23:46'). The feed stacks to the screen edge with no bottom margin matching the command bar. A marker chip pokes out at the left screen edge behind the roster (blue square at x=0 in C2 f00056). Between the siren and the auto-unlock, the Lookout locked shelter in C2 f00040 is a red lock dot jammed between the clock and roster panels.
  • minorIn S7 (all clear, no town hits) 'No impacts in Arad' still shows '0' with no check while the other three are ticked. The debrief then shows it as done, which is inconsistent. capture.json reports hud update 2.5 ms on the sampled frame against a 4 ms whole-frame JS budget, which is too much for a DOM overlay.

Camera & game feel

Building

Intro flyover, input response, hit-stop, alert → shelter → aftermath pacing

r2 scored 6 (r1 5); round 3 building

Open issues 6

  • majorShot F (262-272 s, a third of the trailer) is a near-static wide view across an empty grey plain: three thin smoke columns 6 km away, the moon dead centre and a few trails at the top edge. The camera moves about 40 m/s at 6.5 km range, so there is no parallax and successive frames look identical (f00126, f00156, f00176). A CoH3 or WARNO trailer would never hold 10 s on this; it reads as the clip running out of material.
  • major240-245 s is a slow push over the rooftops toward a horizon with nothing in the sky. Warheads are said to be inbound, but no trail or flash appears until the first Sling launch at about 245 s (f00030). The first 5 s of a trailer are its hook; here they show an empty night town.
  • major0-8 s skims 20 m over a lumpy, uniformly lit surface with a rolled horizon. It reads as flying over a cloud deck, not up a desert wadi: no walls, no rim and no sense of climbing (intro f00010 at t=5). At the crest (9.5-12 s, S1-intro at 11 s) Arad is still a thin band of lights about 3 km off on a featureless plain, sitting directly behind the IRON WALL title. AoE IV and Iron Harvest openers land the reveal on a close, framed subject. Here the reveal only works at 17 s, after the title has gone.
  • majorShots C (250-254) and E (257.6-262) put the camera 3+ km from Arad, so the town is a 3% sliver of lights on the bottom edge. In E the Arrow 3 star-flashes hang in empty sky over a bare plain, with the only ground interest a Nevatim smoke column at the right edge (f00126). Without the defended town under the flashes the stakes vanish, unlike Broken Arrow's interceptions over a skyline.
  • minorIn the trailer the hit-stop distance gate is bypassed (index.js: `|| scriptName === 'trailer'`), so six 55 ms hit-stops fire on kills 8.4-39 km from the camera, and a second slow-mo (Arrow, 258.82) follows 8 s after the Sling one. A freeze for a pinpoint flash 39 km away reads as a dropped frame in the exported video, not impact (TARGET §5: hit-stop must never feel like lag). Two slow-mos in 30 s also dilute the first.
  • minorIn shot D the camera is so low over the flat, sheen-lit plain that the base ground reads like water, and Nevatim shows as scattered dark boxes with no hangar or shelter silhouette near the camera. The impact is strong, but its setting is anonymous.
