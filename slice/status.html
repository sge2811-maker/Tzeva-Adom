Environment & lighting

Critic reviewing

Skyline, atmosphere, fog, tone mapping, bloom, SSAO, colour grade

Round 2 built: warm gradient-noise desert, drainage wadis, carved Dead Sea, Moab ridge layers, outskirts, clean windows, crowd scaling. Critic reviewing.

Open issues 10

  • blockerThe desert does not read as desert. In S1, S3 and S7 the ground is a blue-grey mottled 'cloud sea' or snowfield with leopard-spot blotches, smeared streaks and visible square value-noise cells near the camera (S1 lower-left). The same pattern repeats edge to edge in S2 and S7, which is exactly the procedural repetition TARGET.md lists as a failure. CoH3 and Iron Harvest night maps keep a warm albedo under cool light; this one is cool on cool.
  • blockerThe wadis read as pale topographic contour lines. They are closed, thin, evenly wide loops painted all over the plateau (S2, S7), so the map looks like a survey chart, not eroded ground.
  • majorThe S3 'lookout' vista has no escarpment, no Dead Sea and only one Moab layer. The flat plateau runs straight into a single dark band with a flat, stepped top (S3, S6 crop). The rift haze that should separate plateau | rift | Moab is missing, so there is no TW:WH3-style foreground/mid/far layering. In S7 the Dead Sea is a flat navy rectangle with a straight edge, no moon glint and almost no shoreline lights.
  • majorLit window interiors are filled with scrambled per-pixel 'glyph' noise instead of curtains or rooms. It is very visible at street level (S5 crop) and sparkles at tactical zoom (S4), which reads as a shader bug, not as blinds.
  • majorFrom the default RTS camera (S2) Arad reads as a game board. It is a near-octagonal grid of identical blocks framed by a hard perimeter road and dropped onto empty desert, with no outskirts, industrial edge, fences or dirt tracks. The block interiors are flat, empty beige lots (S4). The buildings are lit cool blue-grey like an overcast day model (S2 and S6 crops: a 'silver glitter' town), not warm stone under sodium light. Compared with CoH3 and the Cities: Skylines II night reference, it lacks warm street-light pools and edge transitions.
  • majorRegional roads are near-black (bluish) ribbons with sharp polyline kinks, no shoulders and a floating look (S1, S2, S7). In S3 the Masada road is a saturated navy strip with crisp edges lying on top of the terrain.

VFX

Building

Launches, interceptor trails, intercepts, debris, smoke, screen shake

Critic R1: 6/10 (also capped by an env console error). Iron Dome launches at WARNO level; ribbons fold edge-on, flat slab ends, Arrow flashes read as fireworks, faint overhead Sling trails, thin aftermath smoke. Round 2 building.

Open issues 9

  • majorRibbon trails twist into flat bow-tie fins with bright seam lines when a trail points toward the camera (S3: the descending trail at the centre corkscrews into visible triangles). The big launch plume shows straight polygon edges on its left flank. Both are the 'primitives' tell that TARGET.md calls out.
  • majorNear the camera, a smoke ribbon renders as a flat, hard-edged grey slab with a square end cap (C5 and S9, lower left). An interceptor's motor streak and a star flash with a hard circular halo edge pass right by the lens at t~253.6.
  • majorArrow 3 exo intercepts (C1, S6) are a sparse cluster of orange re-entry streaks plus a white core inside a large, smooth, round glow disc. They read as fireworks or a lens blob, not a brilliant star-flash over the Dead Sea. The glint cross is almost invisible at 960px. The Arrow climb trails show only as faint blue vertical bands in the upper right, more like searchlight beams than smoke.
  • majorOverhead (C4, S5), the David's Sling trails are thin, pale, scribbled lines. This is the 'trails as thin lines' failure sign, at the signature moment over the town. The Sling intercepts themselves show as round bloom glows with almost no visible fireball or lingering smoke puff.
  • majorS7 aftermath has almost no VFX presence. One faint wispy column with a flat pale dust disc, plus small fires along the escarpment. No tall smoke columns rise over the Negev to sell what happened, whereas CoH3 and WARNO post-battle frames are full of drifting smoke. The S9 ballistic impact cloud is a uniformly orange-lit cotton lump, with no dark smoke above the lit base.
  • minorThe ballistic smoke trail (S9, C5) is a perfectly straight, uniform-width, uniformly lit tan band that reads like a searchlight beam rather than turbulent smoke. In C4 the hot ribbons are constant-width laser lines.
