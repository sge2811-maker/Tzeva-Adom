Environment & lighting

Building

Skyline, atmosphere, fog, tone mapping, bloom, SSAO, colour grade

Critic R2: 5/10 (no change). Warmer and layered (S7 depth, S5 windows near CS2), but the town still reads as a game board at S2/S6, Moab ridges are flat sheets, facades go black under flashes, near ground smudgy or blank. Round 3 building.

Open issues 7

  • blockerFrom the default RTS camera (S2), Arad is still a near-octagon of identical square blocks framed by glowing orange perimeter lines, dropped onto a blank plain. The 'outskirts' are scattered single red and white specks with thin pale scratch lines, which look like debug markers rather than an urban fringe. In S6 the whole town reads as silver-white glitter with flat grey facades. Next to an Age of Empires IV high camera or a Cities: Skylines II night overview, this is the first-glance 'procedural' tell.
  • majorThe Moab layers are vertical flat curtains with uniform navy fill. From the S7 height they read as stacked paper strips with a hard horizontal top and bottom. The farther ridges are darker than the nearer Moab face, which reverses atmospheric perspective. In S6 the ridge crest is visibly stair-stepped, and in S3 and S6 the plateau edge cuts the rift as a ruler-straight horizontal line, so the escarpment never shows.
  • majorA huge intercept flash fills the top of S5, yet every facade is pure black apart from its windows. Only the terrain receives the new flash hemisphere term. Against WARNO and CoH3, where overhead bursts visibly paint roofs and walls, the 'lighting interplay' is missing at the most cinematic moment.
  • majorThe near desert in S1 and in the lower half of S7 is smeared with large dark, soft streaks that read as cloud shadows or soot on snow, and peppered with blue-black 'ink spot' shrub blobs. In S3 the opposite happens: the plateau is a completely featureless beige plane with no micro-relief or material variety for kilometres. Neither reads as Negev hamada next to CoH3 or Iron Harvest ground.
  • majorIn the lookout shot the battery apron reads as a navy-blue swimming pool with a hard dark rim, and the boxy launchers sit in it. The Masada road has saturated orange-tan shoulders as wide as the carriageway, so it looks like masking tape on a tabletop.
  • minorAt tactical zoom the block interiors are large flat beige lots, evenly lit almost like dusk. The new decals are square green or yellow patches that read as placed tiles. The sodium pools exist, but the ground between them isn't dark enough for them to pop the way CoH3 night maps and CS2 do.

VFX

Critic reviewing

Launches, interceptor trails, intercepts, debris, smoke, screen shake

Round 2 built: edge-on ribbon fix, tapered ends, exo star flashes, distance-scaled fireballs, darker longer columns, deterministic RNG. Critic reviewing.

Open issues 9

  • majorRibbon trails twist into flat bow-tie fins with bright seam lines when a trail points toward the camera (S3: the descending trail at the centre corkscrews into visible triangles). The big launch plume shows straight polygon edges on its left flank. Both are the 'primitives' tell that TARGET.md calls out.
  • majorNear the camera, a smoke ribbon renders as a flat, hard-edged grey slab with a square end cap (C5 and S9, lower left). An interceptor's motor streak and a star flash with a hard circular halo edge pass right by the lens at t~253.6.
  • majorArrow 3 exo intercepts (C1, S6) are a sparse cluster of orange re-entry streaks plus a white core inside a large, smooth, round glow disc. They read as fireworks or a lens blob, not a brilliant star-flash over the Dead Sea. The glint cross is almost invisible at 960px. The Arrow climb trails show only as faint blue vertical bands in the upper right, more like searchlight beams than smoke.
  • majorOverhead (C4, S5), the David's Sling trails are thin, pale, scribbled lines. This is the 'trails as thin lines' failure sign, at the signature moment over the town. The Sling intercepts themselves show as round bloom glows with almost no visible fireball or lingering smoke puff.
  • majorS7 aftermath has almost no VFX presence. One faint wispy column with a flat pale dust disc, plus small fires along the escarpment. No tall smoke columns rise over the Negev to sell what happened, whereas CoH3 and WARNO post-battle frames are full of drifting smoke. The S9 ballistic impact cloud is a uniformly orange-lit cotton lump, with no dark smoke above the lit base.
  • minorThe ballistic smoke trail (S9, C5) is a perfectly straight, uniform-width, uniformly lit tan band that reads like a searchlight beam rather than turbulent smoke. In C4 the hot ribbons are constant-width laser lines.
