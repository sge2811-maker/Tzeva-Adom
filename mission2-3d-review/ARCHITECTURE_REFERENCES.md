# Upper Galilee architecture reference pass — 17 October 2026

The isolated mission retains its original, highly compressed tactical positions. These are reference-informed building types and streetscape details, not surveyed replicas, exact addresses, or a geographically accurate map of the three towns. The minimap depicts the playable mission terrain, not Google map data.

## References actually inspected

Google Maps satellite views, opened in Chromium and visually inspected:
- [Metula, HaRishonim / HaNadiv / HaArazim](https://www.google.com/maps/@33.2808,35.5784,18z/data=!3m1!1e3?hl=en): a mix of red pitched roofs and flat roofs, detached buildings in gardens, varied orientations, orchard plots, and low-density street frontage.
- [Kiryat Shmona, Tel Hai / Joseph Trumpeldor / Dan Dayan](https://www.google.com/maps/@33.2077,35.571,18z/data=!3m1!1e3?hl=en): tiled-roof detached neighborhoods alongside larger urban buildings and streets.
- [Shlomi, Efroni / Shahar / Snunit](https://www.google.com/maps/@33.079,35.148,18z/data=!3m1!1e3?hl=en): hillside loops, closely spaced red-roof houses, private gardens, and terraces.

Street-level architectural photographs, downloaded temporarily and visually inspected:
- [Metula street photograph, Zman Israel, March 2025](https://www.zman.co.il/572200/): plaster houses with pitched terracotta roofs, deep eaves, white solar-water tanks and collectors, shutters, AC units, porches, stone garden boundaries, and utility wires. Its post-war damage/overgrowth is not a reference for the mission's October 2023 initial state.
- [Kiryat Shmona street photograph, Davar](https://www.davar1.co.il/570321/): modest multistory apartment buildings, cream/gray finishes, continuous balcony rails, roller shutters, rooftop solar water systems, retaining walls and vegetation.
- [Shlomi Yefe Nof street photograph, Globes, 2022](https://www.globes.co.il/news/article.aspx?did=1001412653): pale residential blocks, balconies, rooftop equipment and substantial stone retaining walls along sloping roads.
- [Beit Shalom, official history/gallery](https://www.beitshalom.co.il/en/gallery/): two historic Hod-Fein houses on HaRishonim, preserved with their original stone. Used as documentary support for the historic stone-house type, not an exact reconstruction of the hotel.

Maps imagery dates are not independently verified. Google Maps indicated that 3D Earth view was unavailable in this cloud browser. No Street View coverage claim is made.

## Implemented art direction

Metula uses a mix of lower stone cottages and newer plaster houses, pitched/hipped terracotta roofs, shutters, timber entrance pergolas, garden walls, gates and scanned shrubs. Kiryat Shmona mixes three-level apartment blocks with detached tiled-roof homes. Shlomi uses pale two-level houses, balconies and stone boundaries. All houses have separate glazing, sills, roof edges/gutters and solar hot-water equipment.

These are compact procedural game assets. Roof geometry, household scale, terrain terraces, house placement and architectural variety still need further refinement before claiming a faithful town recreation or AA quality.

No Google imagery or third-party reference photographs are distributed as game textures. Geometry is newly authored; material textures and scanned foliage retain their existing asset licenses in assets/ATTRIBUTION.md.
